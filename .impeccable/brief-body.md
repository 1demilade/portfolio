## Direction contract

THESIS: A light, colourful, well-made developer portfolio whose proof is the work itself: real screens, real wiring diagrams and real report numbers. It refuses the dark terminal portfolio and the identical project-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cool white ground, indigo-black ink, four saturated fields taken from the projects (Uno blue, L298N red, motor yellow, CocoaCare green), used as whole sections, never as glows. Red Hat Display 900 caps for display, Red Hat Text for reading. Square-ish 6px corners, flat fills, no shadows at rest.

STORY: Who David is in one screen; four projects shown large with their real parts; what he can build for a business; how to reach him.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The name in heavy caps spanning the full width across the middle; role line under it; top-left intro sentence with "See projects" (solid) and "Contact me"; top-right four links; bottom corners location and GitHub/LinkedIn.

FORM: Category standard, executed at full craft (canon, no position on the ordered list). seed:74db72ffe85fb3da. Signature interaction: the live Aso & Thread order builder (colour and size rebuild the real WhatsApp message).
